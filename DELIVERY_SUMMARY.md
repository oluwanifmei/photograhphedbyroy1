## 🚀 How To Get Your Video

### Easiest (Recommended):
1. Go to **https://www.pexels.com/videos/**
2. Search for: "cinematic landscape"
3. Find a video with nature, cities, desert mix
4. Click download (choose 1920x1080)
5. Save to your computer

### Optional: Optimize for Web
1. Download **HandBrake** (free): https://handbrake.fr/
2. Open your video
3. Set bitrate: 5000 kbps
4. Set resolution: 1920x1080
5. Export as MP4
6. File will be 10-25 MB (perfect size)

### Finally: Upload
1. Rename video to: `cinematic-hero.mp4`
2. Place in: `videos/` folder in your website
3. Open `index.html` in browser
4. Celebrate! 🎉
